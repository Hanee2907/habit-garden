# 🌱 Habit Garden – Small habits, big blooms

**🌐 Live demo: https://hanee2907.github.io/habit-garden/**

Habit Garden is a habit tracker where every habit is a plant.
Water it every day and it grows from a seed into a full bloom. Forget it and it slowly wilts.

## 🌼 How plants grow

| Streak | Stage |
|---|---|
| Just planted | 🟤 Seed |
| 1+ days | 🌱 Sprout |
| 3+ days | 🌿 Young plant |
| 7+ days | 🫛 Bud |
| 14+ days | 🌼 Flower |
| 30+ days | 🦋 Full bloom, with butterflies |

**Plants are kind:** miss one day and your plant gets thirsty, but your streak is safe if you water it today.
Miss 3 days and it starts to wilt and lean over. Water it again to bring it back.

## ✨ Features

- 🌻 Four plant types, each drawn with SVG: sunflower, tulip, lavender and cactus
- 🍂 The garden follows the real calendar: spring petals, autumn leaves, winter snow, and fireflies at night
- 🌙 Day and night sky with sun, moon and drifting clouds
- 💧 Watering animation, plus celebration messages when a plant reaches a new stage
- 📊 Garden report: watered today, best streak, total waterings and garden health
- 🗓️ 14-day history heatmap for every habit
- 🧑‍🌾 Gardener level that goes up as you water
- Demo garden for first-time visitors
- Works on phones, tablets and desktops
- Saved in your browser (localStorage)

## 🛠️ Built with

- HTML5
- CSS3 (CSS animations, gradients, responsive grid)
- JavaScript (SVG drawing, streak and date logic, localStorage)
- Fonts: [Baloo 2](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Baloo+2) and [Nunito](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito)

## 🚀 Run it

Download or clone this repo and open `index.html` in your browser. You don't need to install anything.
